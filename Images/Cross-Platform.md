An abstract illustration demonstrating the cross-platform capabilities of the plugin framework, using a dark, high-tech aesthetic with vibrant, glowing elements.

**Central Element: The Plugin Suite**
*   In the center of the composition, floating in dark space, is a cohesive cluster of abstract, geometric shapes. This cluster represents a suite of plugins. It includes a glowing green cube ("Plugin A"), a sharp magenta pyramid ("Plugin B"), and a yellow dodecahedron ("Plugin C"). They are interconnected with soft light, symbolizing that they are a single, unified set of extensions.

**Surrounding Platforms:**
Orbiting the central plugin suite are three distinct "platforms," each represented by a stylized, glowing base on which a holographic OS logo is projected.

1.  **Windows Platform:** On the left, a luminous blue, rectangular base projects the glowing Windows logo above it.
2.  **macOS Platform:** On the right, a sleek, silver-white circular base projects the glowing Apple logo.
3.  **Linux Platform:** At the bottom, a vibrant orange, hexagonal base projects the glowing Tux (penguin) logo.

**The "Run Everywhere" Concept:**
From the central plugin suite, three identical beams of light project outwards, one towards each platform. As a beam hits a platform's base, it materializes a holographic, semi-transparent duplicate of the *entire plugin cluster* above the OS logo.

*   The key visual is that the projected cluster on the Windows platform is identical to the one on the macOS platform and the one on the Linux platform. The colors, shapes, and arrangement of the holographic plugins (green cube, magenta pyramid, yellow dodecahedron) are exactly the same across all three.

**Overall Impression:**
The illustration powerfully communicates the "write once, run anywhere" nature of the framework. The single, central source of plugins being projected identically onto different operating systems provides an immediate and intuitive understanding of its cross-platform design. The dark background and glowing, high-contrast colors give it a modern and sophisticated feel.
