**Title:** Logo for "Lightweight C++ Plugin Framework"

**Prompt for Image Generation:**

**Core Subject:** A minimalist and professional logo for a "Lightweight C++ Plugin Framework". The design must convey concepts of a central core, modular plugins, and connectivity.

**Visual Style:**
*   **Aesthetic:** Flat design or 2.5D (subtle gradients and soft shadows). Clean, modern, and high-tech vector graphic.
*   **Color Palette:** A professional tech palette. Use a dark navy blue or charcoal as the primary color for the main element. Use a contrasting vibrant accent color, like electric blue or bright orange, for connecting elements or a single "active" plugin. Use white or a very light gray for highlights if needed.
*   **Simplicity:** The logo should be simple enough to be recognizable when small (e.g., as a favicon). Avoid overly complex details.

**Composition & Elements:**
1.  **Central Core:** A solid, geometric shape, preferably a hexagon, representing the core framework. It should be the largest element.
2.  **Plugins:** Two or three smaller, identical geometric shapes (e.g., smaller hexagons or squares) positioned near the central core. These represent the plugins.
3.  **Connection:** Clean, thin lines or paths originating from the faces of the central core and connecting to the plugin shapes. These lines represent the API/connection.
4.  **Arrangement:** Arrange the plugins asymmetrically around the core to create a dynamic feel, but keep the overall logo balanced. One plugin could be highlighted with the accent color. The entire composition should feel cohesive, as if the parts form a single, elegant new shape.

**Negative Space:** Clever use of negative space is a plus. For example, the arrangement of shapes could subtly form the letter 'P' for 'Plugin' or 'C' for 'C++'.

**Examples of what to avoid:**
*   Avoid literal puzzle pieces, as they can look cliché.
*   Do not include any text in the logo itself.
*   Avoid overly bright, garish colors.
*   No photorealism; this must be a clean graphic.

**Overall Impression:** The final logo should look sharp, sophisticated, and instantly communicate the idea of a stable core being extended by smaller, interconnected modules. It should be suitable for a modern software project on GitHub.
